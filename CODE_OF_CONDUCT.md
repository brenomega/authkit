# Code of conduct

## Our standard

AuthKit contributors and maintainers must provide a respectful, harassment-free environment regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, accountability, and attention to the security and privacy of others. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, insults, sexualized attention, stalking, doxxing, deliberate exposure of secrets or vulnerabilities, and sustained disruption.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate it, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately where appropriate, and avoid exposing reporters or affected people.

Report routine conduct concerns to the maintainer through a private GitHub contact channel. If the report includes a security vulnerability or sensitive operational detail, use the private vulnerability-reporting link in `SECURITY.md`. Public issues must not contain confidential reports. Community response is best-effort and has no guaranteed response time.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by the Contributor Covenant and adapted to the project's current single-maintainer governance.
