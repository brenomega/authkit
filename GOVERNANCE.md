# Governance

Breno (`brenomega`) is the initial maintainer and final decision-maker for AuthKit. The maintainer sets release scope, reviews and merges pull requests, coordinates security disclosures, appoints future maintainers, and alone authorizes release promotion and tags.

Contributions are accepted through pull requests under Apache License 2.0 and the Developer Certificate of Origin 1.1. No Contributor License Agreement is required initially. Technical decisions should favor the normative public contract, security standards, executable evidence, backward compatibility within `0.1.x`, and an honest support boundary.

The maintainer may delegate review or operational duties without transferring final release authority. If more maintainers are appointed, this document will record their roles, decision process, conflict handling, and succession rules. Project name use under the license must not imply official endorsement of a fork.
